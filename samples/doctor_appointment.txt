As a patient, I want to book doctor appointments on the health platform and sync them with my Google or Outlook calendar, so that my appointments stay up to date across tools.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Patients can view a doctor's available slots (from the doctor's synced calendar plus manual inputs)
- Patients select a time slot and confirm with a single click
- A confirmation email and calendar invite are sent on booking
- Doctors authorize calendar access once via OAuth2 (Google and Microsoft); tokens are stored encrypted and refreshed
- Busy slots read from the doctor's calendar are marked unavailable
- Confirmed appointments are written to the doctor's calendar
- Patients and doctors can cancel or reschedule; sync updates reflect within 5 minutes
- Conflict checks are required before confirmation
- Must comply with HIPAA; a consent log is maintained for each doctor's authorization
- Email reminders are sent 24h and 1h before the appointment

Edge Conditions:
- Calendar token expires and requires a resync flow
- Timezone mismatch falls back to the patient's timezone
- Doctor deletes a synced calendar event, so the appointment must be flagged
